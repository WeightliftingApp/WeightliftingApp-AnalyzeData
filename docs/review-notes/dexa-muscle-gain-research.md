# Primary research for the DEXA muscle-gain estimate

## Bottom line

DEXA lean soft tissue is not skeletal muscle. It is the non-fat, non-bone-mineral soft-tissue compartment assigned by the scanner. Water, glycogen, organs, skin, and connective tissue all contribute. Appendicular lean soft tissue is a better muscle proxy because the limbs contain a high proportion of skeletal muscle, but it is still a proxy.

For serial total lean measurements on the same scanner, `0.76 kg` per scan is a defensible error standard deviation. It comes from 609 repeat Hologic whole-body scans taken 3 to 51 days apart. For the difference between two scans, that implies a 95% least-significant change of about `2.77 * 0.76 = 2.11 kg`, or `4.6 lb`. This estimate captures more day-to-day variation than duplicate scans performed minutes apart. It does not cover a scanner change or a deliberate dehydration or carbohydrate-loading protocol. [Powers et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24200863/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2013.09.010)

No primary study supports converting an individual's change in total DEXA lean soft tissue to skeletal muscle with one universal fraction. A transparent estimator should report observed lean change first, then label muscle gain as an interpretation. Regional DEXA can set the central interpretation, while strength and training history can corroborate its direction. They cannot turn pounds added to a lift into pounds of muscle.

## Evidence and implications

| Question | Primary evidence | What it supports here |
|---|---|---|
| How large is ordinary serial-scan error? | In 609 NHANES participants scanned twice 3 to 51 days apart, total lean mass had RMS-SD `0.760 kg` and RMS-CV `1.46%`. BMC RMS-SD was `27.3 g`; fat mass RMS-SD was `0.505 kg`. Precision varied with BMI and sex. [Powers et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24200863/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2013.09.010) | Use `sigma_scan = 0.76 kg` for the main total-lean error model. Two independent endpoints then have change SD `sqrt(2) * 0.76 kg` and a 95% span of about `+/-2.11 kg`. |
| What is the scanner and repositioning floor? | Two consecutive, repositioned GE Lunar iDXA scans in 52 adults produced total-lean RMS-SD `0.244 kg`, CV `0.5%`, and BMC RMS-SD `0.015 kg`. [Hind et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20842171/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ejcn.2010.190) | A same-session two-scan LSC of about `0.68 kg` is an optimistic sensitivity case. It omits day-to-day biological variation. |
| Does regional DEXA have useful precision? | Duplicate scans in 60 Division I athletes gave total-lean 95% LSCs of `0.575 kg` in men and `0.381 kg` in women. Individual-limb lean LSCs ranged from `0.151` to `0.460 kg`, with larger relative error in smaller regions. [Buehring et al., 2014](https://uwnmbl.engr.wisc.edu/pubs/JCD13_buehring.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2013.02.012) | Appendicular change is useful as a sensitivity check, but its four limb values are not four independent confirmations of muscle gain. Keep ROI and positioning rules fixed. |
| How much can hydration and glycogen move lean mass? | Twelve active men lost `1.93 kg` of body mass and `1.69 kg` of DEXA lean tissue after exercise and heat dehydration. After 48 hours of carbohydrate supercompensation, body mass rose `2.53 kg` and lean tissue rose `2.36 kg`, with no change in fat or BMC. [Toomey et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28204901/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-017-3552-x) | Different hydration and glycogen states can mimic several pounds of lean change without new muscle protein. Use matched, rested, fasted scans when possible. |
| Is glycogen alone material? | In 18 trained male cyclists, glycogen loading raised total lean estimates by `2.0%` to `3.0%` and leg lean by `2.6%` to `3.1%`. Glycogen depletion lowered leg lean by `1.4%`; total body water rose under loading conditions. [Bone et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28410328/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001174) | A muscular, trained subject can move DEXA lean values through substrate and water changes alone. Do not read every lean pound as new contractile tissue. |
| Does food and gut content matter? | Forty-eight adults completed high- and low-carbohydrate diet conditions. Afternoon feeding increased average total and regional DEXA lean soft tissue by as much as `1.7%` and `3%`; individual increases exceeded `4.5%` and `9%`. Values returned to baseline after an overnight fast. [Shiel et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27875499/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001148) | Time of day, fasting, and recent intake belong in the scan protocol. Ingested mass can be allocated to lean tissue. |
| Can appendicular lean estimate skeletal muscle level? | In 321 model-development and 93 validation adults, MRI total skeletal muscle and DEXA appendicular lean soft tissue had `R^2 = 0.96`. The full level equation was `SM = 1.13 * ALST - 0.02 * age + 0.61 * sex + 0.97`, with SEE `1.58 kg`. [Kim et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12145010/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/76.2.378) A later 475-person study obtained `SM = 1.12 * ALM - 0.63`, with validation RMSE `1.74 kg`. [McCarthy et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9929067/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-29827-y) | The stable `1.12` to `1.13` slope makes `1.12 * appendicular-lean change` a reasonable named sensitivity case. Both equations predict levels across people. Neither validates that slope for change within one person. |
| Does DXA lean change track MRI muscle change? | After 10 weeks of resistance training in 26 adults, thigh DXA lean and MRI muscle measures correlated `r = 0.89` cross-sectionally but only `r = 0.49` for percentage change. The 95% agreement limits for change were `-10.61%` to `+10.19%`, and only 46% of scans agreed within 3%. [Tavoian et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6624257/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-46428-w) In ten male sumo wrestlers followed for about two years, change agreement was good for legs but poor for the trunk. [Midorikawa et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29703658/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2018.03.008) | Longitudinal validation is mixed and small. Give appendicular or leg change more weight than trunk change, but do not claim MRI-like precision. The Tavoian limits are a stress test, not a portable error distribution for this record. |
| Do device and protocol changes matter? | Forty adults scanned consecutively on GE Lunar Prodigy and Hologic Horizon had mean ALM differences of about `1.8 kg` in men and `1.0 kg` in women, despite high correlation. [Park et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8743584/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3803/EnM.2021.1274) In 38 men on one GE Lunar iDXA, changing hand position shifted total lean by `0.11 kg` and altered regional precision. [Thurlow et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28462788/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2017.03.003) | Treat a scanner or manufacturer change as a systematic bias. Split the time series into comparable-device windows or add a separate bias term. Keep positioning and analysis mode consistent. |
| Does body size change precision? | Duplicate GE Lunar Prodigy scans in 144 women found total-lean RMS-SD of `0.551 kg` at normal BMI, `0.540 kg` when overweight, and `0.781 kg` with obesity. [Knapp et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25087044/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocd.2014.06.001) | Larger absolute error at larger body size is plausible. A large weight change also changes non-muscle lean compartments, so total lean change should not receive a fixed muscle fraction without regional support. |

## Recommended uncertainty model

Keep measurement and interpretation uncertainty separate.

1. Model each observed total lean value as `LST_observed = LST_true + error`, with `error ~ Normal(0, 0.76 kg)`. For an earliest-to-latest difference, this produces a 95% measurement interval of about `+/-2.11 kg`, or `+/-4.6 lb`. If the estimator fits all scans, attach the error to each scan rather than adding `+/-2.11 kg` after the fit.
2. Do not add the full dehydration, feeding, and glycogen shifts on top of `0.76 kg` by default. The serial-scan estimate already contains ordinary biological variation. Use the acute studies to justify the conservative error choice and widen it only when scan preparation clearly differed.
3. Report `Delta LST` as the observed result. Do not relabel it as muscle.
4. If appendicular lean is available across the window, show `1.12 * Delta ALM` as a sensitivity estimate of total skeletal-muscle change. Label the `1.12` coefficient as cross-sectional. Use the regional direction and magnitude to choose among interpretation cases, not to erase uncertainty.
5. If only total lean is available, use an explicit judgment factor, such as `Delta muscle = q * Delta LST_true`, and show several `q` cases. Values such as `0.50`, `0.75`, and `1.00` are scenarios, not literature-derived probabilities. The report should say so.
6. Do not average total lean and appendicular estimates as independent observations. Appendicular lean is part of total lean. Use it to move the central case or widen the interval.
7. If scanner identity changed, do not use the same-device normal error model across that break. A separate systematic offset or device-specific window is required.

The main 95% interval should be at least as wide as the serial-scan measurement interval after conversion to pounds. It should be wider when the muscle interpretation is uncertain. Report the result to the nearest `0.5 lb`; the studies do not support finer precision.

## Evidence gaps

- There is no validated universal ratio from longitudinal total DEXA lean change to skeletal-muscle change in trained adults.
- Cross-sectional appendicular equations predict muscle levels well at the group level. Their residual error does not translate cleanly into a within-person change error because stable anatomical differences may cancel.
- Longitudinal DXA-versus-MRI studies are small, use different regions and time windows, and disagree on individual-level performance.
- Published precision is scanner-, software-, operator-, population-, and protocol-specific. The `0.76 kg` value is a reasonable external prior, not a substitute for duplicate scans on the actual device.
- Strength and training volume support the plausibility and timing of hypertrophy. No source here justifies converting strength gain or tonnage into muscle mass.

## Measurement that would narrow the interval most

A standardized repeat body-composition visit would help more than another training metric. The strongest design is two rested, morning, overnight-fasted scans with matched hydration, carbohydrate intake, recent exercise, scanner, software, positioning, and ROI placement. Pair DEXA with MRI muscle volume of the thighs and upper arms if the goal is skeletal muscle rather than lean tissue. Duplicate DEXA constrains measurement error; MRI constrains the lean-to-muscle interpretation.
